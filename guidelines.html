Rental Guidelines & Policy

🔒 Security Deposit & Payment

  • A ₱600 deposit is required for all rentals.
  • Rental fees and deposit must be paid in full prior to or at pickup.
  • Deposit is fully refundable after a clean post-use inspection 💸
  • Valid, government-issued ID required upon pickup 🆔
  • No Sub-Letting: Rented dresses are strictly for the named renter 🚫

👗 Dress Care & Safety

  • Select proper dress size and heel height to avoid tripping hazards 👠
  • No unauthorized alterations (cutting, glue, or iron-on tape) ✂️
  • Avoid open flames, fireworks, or hazardous environments 🔥

⏰ Returns & Late Policy

  • Items must be returned by the agreed date & time.
  • Late Fees: Unapproved late returns incur a ₱100/hour penalty ⏳
  • Extensions: ₱300 fee applies (must request 3 hrs prior; subject to availability) 📲
  • Return items clean, undamaged, and complete with original packaging 🛍️

⚠️ Damage, Loss & Liability

  • Renters cover repair or deep cleaning costs from misuse or negligence 🧼
  • Full replacement cost charged for loss or irreparable damage 🏷️
  • Customer assumes operational risk and holds shop harmless against personal injury or property damage 🛡️
