RENTAL GUIDELINES & POLICY

🔒 Security Deposit & Payment

  • A ₱600 deposit is required for all rentals.
  • Rental fees and deposit must be paid in full prior to or at pickup.
  • Deposit is fully refundable after a clean post-use inspection
  • 1 Valid, government-issued ID required upon pickup
  • No Subletting: Rented dresses are strictly for the named renter

👗 Dress Care & Safety

  • Select proper dress size and heel height to avoid tripping hazards.
  • No unauthorized alterations (e.g., cutting, glue, or iron-on tape).
  • Avoid open flames, fireworks, or hazardous environments.
  • DO NOT LAUNDER OR DRY-CLEAN THE DRESS. Cleaning is managed by Fey Co. Rental.

⏰ Returns & Late Policy

  • Items must be returned by the agreed date and time.
    • Late Fees: Unapproved late returns incur a ₱100/hour penalty ⌛
    • Extensions: A ₱300 fee applies (must be requested 3 hrs prior; subject to availability)
  • Return items clean, undamaged, and complete with their original packaging

⚠️ Damage, Loss & Liability

  • Renters cover repair or deep cleaning costs resulting from misuse or negligence
    • Deep Cleaning Fee: A ₱200-500 fee will be charged depending on the dress design, applicable if the rented item requires deep cleaning after use
  • Renters are responsible for any damage, stains, excessive dirt, or loss of the rental packaging, including the dustbag
    • Packaging and Dust Bag Fees: May apply depending on the extent of the damage or replacement cost
    • Full replacement cost will be charged for loss or irreparable damage
  • Customer assumes all operational risk and holds the shop harmless against personal injury or property damage
